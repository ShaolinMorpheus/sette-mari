What the game stores

Saved games, settings (sound, language, controls) and the name you choose for the Captain are stored only on your device. No account is needed and I do not receive this data.

Ads

The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob (an optional rewarded video and, now and then, a full-screen ad between chapters). Google may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and technical information to serve and measure ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent at first launch; you can change it from the game menu (Game → Privacy settings). You can also reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android settings. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Captains' Board (web version only)

If you write a message on the Captains' Board, the text and the name you enter are sent to me through a website form so I can read ideas and bug reports. Please do not write personal data.

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.
